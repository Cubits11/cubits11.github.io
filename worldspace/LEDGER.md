# Experiment E4 — WORLDSPACE

DEFEAT → DATA → ADAPTATION → NEW EXPERIMENT → SUCCESS, the same ledger law the
films keep. Nothing here is deleted; a wrong prediction is the useful row.
Grading is of pixels and of people, never of code or intent.

## Standing tension, stated before anything else

`docs/FRONTIER_ROADMAP.md` names a non-goal: *more dashboards, visualizations,
or social copy for results already stated.* WORLDSPACE shows a result already
stated (CC-001, CC-004). It is licensed as an experiment only because its
changed variable is not the picture but the **operation the visitor performs**,
and because its measurement is not "it exists" but cold comprehension against
a frozen protocol. If the cold test shows no gain over the film or Fig. 02,
the non-goal wins and this ledger records it.

## Prediction (written before the first render, 2026-09-02)

Direct manipulation plus prediction-before-revelation will produce clearer
cold understanding of non-identifiability than passive explanation, while
retaining correct scope: viewers will (a) name the two scores as fixed and
the joint count as what moved, (b) say the example is constructed, not a
measurement, (c) tie the 1% point to independence or to multiplying the
scores, and (d) name the script, the claim, or an independent check as what
they would do next.

**What would count against it**

- repeated confusion about constructed vs empirical (Q2 fails);
- failure to name what stayed fixed (Q1 fails);
- reading independence as the true answer (Q3 fails);
- the interaction obscuring rather than clarifying the proof (Q1 fails
  *more* often than on the film or Fig. 02);
- a static poster outperforming the instrument on the same questions;
- the phone interaction failing (a viewer cannot move a ring or the count).

**Success is not** "WORLDSPACE exists." Deployment proves availability.
Understanding is the measurement; an independent rerun of TRY-A is the
consequence that would matter.

Two representations were killed *inside* V10 after they were drawn on paper:

- **a histogram of column sizes** (the counting measure is far from uniform:
  5.7 × 10¹² arrangements at 0, 7.1 × 10¹² at 1, one at 10) — killed because a
  shape over the interval reads as likelihood (H11), which is exactly the
  claim the instrument exists to withhold; equal columns state attainment and
  nothing else;
- **an autoplaying sweep** through the eleven worlds — killed by the first
  law: the visitor's own act must produce the change (H3, H9).

## Selected grammar — SPECIMEN AND FIELD

One paper sheet (the master film's arrangement, same seed) the visitor
manipulates; then a pull-back into a black field where that sheet is one
specimen among 154 shown, all printing the same scorecard, placed in eleven
columns over the identified axis; the two scores in a band that never leaves
the screen; one amber point; one switch; one command.

Visible object → meaning is the `objects` block of `manifest.yaml`; every
entry has a status. Nothing on the stage lacks an entry.

## What survived, what was removed

**Motion that survived, each carrying information:** a ring's hop (the
arrangement changes); the BOTH MISS numeral (the joint changes); the
pull-back (one sheet becomes a specimen among many); the amber point's
landing (an assumption arrives); the collapse and reopening of the field
(the assumption selects, then releases); the scroll that brings a control
the visitor caused into view. Under reduced motion each is a cut.

**Removed before shipping:** the starfield that was never drawn; the
histogram; the autoplay sweep; a hover inspector (no hover required); the
BELLS second chapter (Act V) — routed instead to experiment B with three bound
numbers, because one profound interaction beats two compressed lectures; the
h1 subtitle on phones; per-world atom vectors on the stage (they live in the
static proof and one click away).

## Untested, and therefore not claimed

- **Any human.** Zero cold viewers. Availability is proven; communication
  effectiveness is not.
- **A real assistive technology session** (VoiceOver / NVDA). The DOM carries
  labels, a live region, a range input and a switch; that approximates and
  does not replace an AT pass — the same debt the site's design ledger already
  records.
- **Real touch hardware.** Pointer events were exercised in an emulated
  mobile context with a mouse; tap-to-hop and the range input are the
  guaranteed paths.
- **Sound.** The opt-in tones were not graded by ear on hardware.

## Cold-test protocol

Frozen in `manifest.yaml → cold_test` (five questions, scoring rules, HOLD
rule) before any viewer answers. Responses go verbatim into
`distribution/outcomes.yaml → diagnostics.cold_comprehension_trials` with
`film: worldspace`. Scoring is not rewritten after responses.

## Next experiment

Cold humans (5–8) encounter WORLDSPACE without prior explanation, sound off,
and answer the five frozen questions. That is the measurement this
intervention was built for. Nothing else is proposed.
