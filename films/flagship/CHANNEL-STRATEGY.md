## What would change this strategy

If people choose to watch but leave during the explanation, shorten or simplify the transition where the actual retention curve drops. If they stay through the proof but do not continue, test the destination and its promise. If few choose to watch, test the immediate question and visible action before changing the research topic. If the sample remains too small or processing incomplete, preserve uncertainty and keep collecting comparable observations.

The differentiator is not a claim to match another creator's quality. It is allowing the audience to see a claim become narrower, a count change, and a correction remain visible. Spend the visual budget on those moments.
