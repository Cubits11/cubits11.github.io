## The trade, stated before writing

The evidence-ledger standing rule says name the trade before adding another
governing document. This is the third governing document written this week
against a repository with zero observation rows, so the bar is high and I will
not pretend otherwise.

**What this does not do.** It produces no observation row, clears no K1, and
does not move `distribution/QUEUE.md` item 5 — five cold viewers, still the
next human action, still not run.

**Why write it anyway.** The site has a specific, diagnosable failure that no
amount of further rigour fixes. Its central number is *0 of 20 evaluations
document matched operating thresholds together with full exposure.* To
understand that sentence a reader must already hold at least eight prior
concepts — what a denominator is, what a threshold does, what exposure means,
that two rates do not fix an overlap, that evidence can permit a set rather
than a point. The site assumes all eight and teaches none. A stranger does not
disagree with MC-001; **they cannot parse it.** That is not a rigour problem
and it will not be solved by another verifier.

**Why it is at risk of becoming the disease.** Every previous instinct here has
been to build apparatus. A teaching layer is apparatus. The specific guard is
in §8: the program has a count, the count starts at 1, and it has kill
conditions that fire before it can grow into a platform.

**What it explicitly does not authorize.** A course product, a community, a
newsletter, certificates, accounts, streaks, monetisation, or any of it. That
is already barred by `distribution/QUEUE.md` and it stays barred. This is a
prerequisite layer on an existing site, or it is nothing.

## 1. Novelty statement — read this before claiming anything

**Nothing in this program is a new result.** Everything mathematical is old:
Fréchet (1951) and Hoeffding (1940) for the bounds; Bonferroni for the lower
endpoint; classical partial identification for the framing.

Everything pedagogical is also old, and I am naming it so the boundary is
visible rather than convenient:

- Physical manipulables for abstract structure — Montessori; Dienes' blocks
  and his *perceptual* and *mathematical variability* principles; base-ten
  materials. Decades old.
- The idea that a manipulable should be structurally true to what it models —
  discussed in mathematics education under names like **structural fidelity**
  and **epistemic fidelity**. Also old.
- Concreteness fading — Fyfe and colleagues. Old.
- Formative assessment, hinge questions, ICAP — Black & Wiliam; Leahy, Lyon,
  Thompson & Wiliam; Chi & Wylie.

*Every citation in this section is from memory. None was reopened while
writing. Treat them as pointers to check, not as support.*

**What is claimed, and it is claimed only as creativity:**

1. The specific devices in `devices.yaml` — this arrangement of household
   objects mapped onto *these* estimands. A bead cube for CC-003. A ruler on a
   sorted line for matched thresholds. A Sudoku cell for a finite identified
   set. Those specific mappings are the creative work and nothing more.
2. **An admission gate with a falsifier** (§2). Structural fidelity as an
   *idea* is old. Stating it as a condition a device passes or fails, with a
   named refutation anyone can attempt — exhibit a reachable configuration that
   is not a feasible world — is the form this repository already uses on
   claims, applied to teaching objects.
3. The count in §8 as the program's only success metric.

If someone shows that (2) is a standard published gate under another name, the
correct response is to adopt their name and drop the claim. That is the
falsifier for this section.

## 2. The device spec — what makes something Necromancer-grade

The Necromancer is the existing device and the reason this program exists.
Twenty coins. Ten are A's misses, ten are B's misses. *Before* any exposition:
"slide them to show the best stack, and the worst stack." Overlap them fully and
you have built the dependent world. Interleave them and you have built
independence. Their hands are the observation.

Extracting what makes it work, as six criteria:

| # | Criterion | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **N1** | **Pre-verbal.** The learner acts before any exposition. | Is there a single sentence of explanation before the first hand movement? If yes, fail. |
| **N2** | **Faithful.** Reachable configurations correspond to the feasible set — every reachable configuration is a legal world, and every legal world is reachable. Surjective-not-injective is allowed if the kernel is stated. | Try to build an illegal world with your hands. Try to name a legal world you cannot build. |
| **N3** | **Readout.** The configuration names the learner's assumption without them having to articulate it. | Can you diagnose from the object alone, with the learner silent? |
| **N4** | **Two-sided error.** At least two distinct wrong configurations exist, naming *different* misconceptions. | A device with one wrong answer is a quiz. |
| **N5** | **Cheap.** Household objects, under 60 seconds to set up, no screen required. | Could a stranger do this at a kitchen table tonight? |
| **N6** | **Carries its own limit.** What the device cannot show is visible in the device. | Name the estimand it must never be used for, and check that the object makes that obvious. |

### The ladder

Four rungs after the §10 audit, so honest failures have somewhere to sit and so
self-assessment cannot masquerade as verification.

- **FAITHFUL** — all six, **audited by someone other than the designer.** This
  is what the program counts.
- **SELF-AUDITED** — all six by the designer's own reckoning, no independent
  audit. Added by loophole L4. It is where every device on this site sits today,
  including the Necromancer.
- **ILLUSTRATIVE** — N1, N3, N5 hold; **N2 fails or is not defined.** Genuinely
  useful, must never be presented as showing the structure is *forced*.
- **DEMONSTRATION** — N1 fails. It is a picture. Allowed, never counted.

Faithfulness is always **relative to stated parameters** (L3). The Necromancer
is faithful *at marginals (0.10, 0.10) with twenty coins*; change the marginals
and the coin count must change with them. Write `FAITHFUL(θ)`, never
`FAITHFUL`.

### The falsifier for any device

> **Exhibit a reachable configuration that is not a feasible world, or a
> feasible world with no reachable configuration.** Either one drops the device
> from FAITHFUL to ILLUSTRATIVE. The rung is recorded; the device is not
> deleted.

This is the whole gate, and it is why Swiss cheese fails: the holes are drawn
independent, so the arrangement space of the picture is smaller than the
feasible set. A learner cannot build the dependent world out of it. It fails N2
and it fails N6, because nothing in the picture tells you what it is hiding.

### Presentations are not devices

The coin slide and the paper-strip overlap are **one device in two
presentations** — same state space, same readout, same failure modes. They
count once. This rule exists specifically to stop the count inflating, which is
the only way this program can lie.

## 3. The prerequisite ladder — derived, not listed

Derived by taking the site's hardest public sentence and asking what a reader
must already hold to parse it.

> *"As of 2026-08-27, among 20 public evaluations meeting frozen criteria, 0
> document matched operating thresholds together with full exposure."*

Ten atoms. A1–A4 are load-bearing for that sentence; A5–A10 are load-bearing
for the claims it sits next to.

| # | Atom | Without it, the reader… |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | A rate is a count over a population | hears "66%" as a property of a thing |
| A2 | Two rates do not fix their overlap | multiplies, and believes the product |
| A3 | A threshold turns a score into a decision | thinks "catch rate" is intrinsic |
| A4 | Exposure — who actually saw the item | applies static arithmetic to a routed system |
| A5 | A composition rule must be named | thinks "stacked" means one thing |
| A6 | A bound is an answer; a point inside it is not | reads an interval as indecision |
| A7 | Evidence can permit a *set* | asks "but what's the real number" |
| A8 | All-miss is not the complement of "good" | hears union success, not residual risk |
| A9 | Pairs do not determine triples | thinks more pairwise testing closes it |
| A10 | A falsifier is written before the outcome | reads a correction as weakness |

A1 → A2 → A6 → A7 is the spine. A3 → A4 is the branch that reaches MC-001's
zero. A9 and A10 are leaves and can be reached in any order.

## 4. The devices

Full records in [`devices.yaml`](devices.yaml). Design notes on the ones that
carry the most weight.

### D-002 · The Necromancer (A2, A6) — the only one that exists

Twenty coins, ten and ten, slide to show best and worst. **FAITHFUL.** Reachable
overlaps are exactly 0 through 10 coins, which is exactly the Fréchet interval
at those marginals, and no hand position produces 11 or −1. N4: overlapping
fully names the dependence chunk; interleaving names independence-as-default;
freezing names "I think one of these numbers is missing."

N6 is why it must never be used for exposure: every coin is on the table and
every coin is seen by both, so the device *silently assumes* full exposure. That
is exactly why A4 needs its own device and cannot be a variation of this one.

### D-004 · The Sorting Line (A3, and the top underdog)

Twenty objects laid out in size order. A ruler laid across the line is the
threshold: everything past it is "caught." Two people, two rulers, same
objects. Two different catch rates from one identical population.

Ask: *"which detector is better?"* If they answer without first moving the two
rulers to the same place, they have the unmatched-threshold bug, and they have
it in their hands where they can see it.

**This is the device I care most about.** Matched operating thresholds is the
least cinematic object on the site and the one that makes four other objects
mean anything, and MC-001's zero is that concept made countable. Four seconds
with a ruler turns it from a phrase into a physical act. **FAITHFUL** — the
reachable configurations are exactly the monotone thresholds on the ordering,
which is exactly the family a score-based detector has.

Extension for the census: lay twenty rulers on twenty copies of the line, none
at the same position, and say *nobody wrote down where they put it.* That is
0 of 20, physically.

### D-007 · The Bead Cube (A9) — CC-003 in your hand

A transparent 2×2×2 box, eight cells, four beads. Two arrangements:

- **Even:** `000, 011, 101, 110`
- **Odd:** `001, 010, 100, 111`

Look at the box from each of the three faces. Every face shows the same 2×2
shadow — one bead behind each of the four positions — **in both arrangements.**
Every pair agrees. Every single agrees.

Now look at the far corner, `111`. One arrangement has a bead there. The other
does not. Zero versus one quarter, and it is a corner you can point at.

**FAITHFUL**, and it is the CC-003 construction exactly, not an analogy for it.
N6: the arrangements are constructed, so the device shows a gap *can* exist and
must never be used to suggest how large the gap typically is.

### D-006 · The Sudoku Cell (A7) — the widest doorway in the program

A 4×4 Latin-square fragment whose givens leave one cell with exactly three legal
values. Ask them to write down the answer for that cell. They will write three
numbers, unprompted, without distress, and without needing the phrase
*identified set.*

**Millions of people already hold partial-identification intuition and do not
know it has a name.** They have narrowed a cell to a short list a thousand
times, and never once felt that a three-value list was a failure to answer.
Then: MC-003's identified set on the BELLS harmful stratum is
`{0/82 … 12/82}` — a cell narrowed to thirteen legal values by the constraints,
with 9 sitting inside it.

**FAITHFUL** for a discrete identified set, and the cheapest doorway on the
site. N6: Sudoku has a unique global solution and this problem does not — say
so out loud, or the device teaches that more evidence always resolves to a
point, which is the opposite of the lesson.

### D-005 · The Second Reader (A4)

One page of text, two proofreaders. Two runs.

- **Full exposure:** both read the whole page.
- **Routed:** reader B receives only the lines A marked — hand over a physical
  slip, not the page.

The exposure regime is *the piece of paper you hand over.* Compute a static OR
after handing the slip and the error is not abstract; it is on the table, in the
wrong shape.

**FAITHFUL** for the two-regime distinction (state space = which artifact is
handed over). N4: computing static OR on the routed handoff names one bug;
saying "routed is the same but cheaper" names another.

### D-001 · The Two Jars (A1)

Two jars, three red marbles each, one holding ten marbles and one holding a
hundred. *"Which is worse?"* If they answer without emptying a jar, they have
the half-a-number bug. **FAITHFUL**, and thirty seconds.

### D-003 · The Two Hasps (A5)

A box with two hasps. Wire it so both must open, or so either opens. The learner
sets the hardware when told *"make it safer."*

Note the reversal: two locks was **rejected** as a metaphor for the Fréchet
episode precisely because it imports series composition. Here that import is the
lesson. Same object, wrong for one atom, exactly right for another. That the
spec distinguishes these is evidence the spec is doing work rather than
decorating.

### D-008 · The Double-Marked Cards (A8)

Cards; each reader marks the ones they missed. Count cards carrying *every*
mark. Union is the pile with at least one mark, and the two piles are visibly
different sizes. **FAITHFUL.**

### D-009 · The Sealed Envelope (A10)

Write the condition that would make you wrong, seal it, date it, leave it
visible. Run the count. Then ask them to write the condition *after* seeing the
result and notice how much easier it is.

**ILLUSTRATIVE, not FAITHFUL** — it is temporal, not configurational, so N2 is
not even defined for it. Recorded at the lower rung rather than argued up. If
every device came out FAITHFUL the ladder would be decoration.

### D-010 · The Five Receipts (A1 extension)

Five receipts, one dinner, five totals. Recover which meal each describes by
finding the smallest whole number of diners that makes every per-person share a
whole number of cents. **ILLUSTRATIVE** — it teaches the recovery trick
honestly, but the reachable space is not the feasible space of denominators, so
it does not clear N2. Do not argue it up.

## 5. The interface — the inversion

The comparison the owner reached for is Khan Academy. The relevant thing about
Khan Academy for this material is what to *invert*: it explains, then tests.
This program's own source material says an assessment that does not change the
next move is assessment theatre, and a video that explains before reading the
learner is high-bandwidth emission into a channel nobody read.

So:

> **The site does not explain. It reads, then chooses.**

Nine rules, all of which must survive a static build.

1. **No prose above the fold.** A node opens with the device instruction and one
   hinge question. Nothing else.
2. **Nothing plays until something moves.** No video, no animation, no
   exposition before an answer is submitted.
3. **No "correct."** The response is a readout, not a verdict: *"That answer
   means you assumed the two failures are unrelated. Here is where that
   assumption is."* Never a judgment about the person. This is not politeness —
   a verdict ends the diagnosis, and the answer is the observation.
4. **No score, no streak, no badge, no percentage complete.** Those are the
   fluency bug in gamified form: they make progress *feel* measured. Progress is
   instead the list of sentences you can now restate, which is the actual
   success criterion and doubles as the retrieval ledger.
5. **Branch, always.** Getting it right skips forward. It does not replay the
   explanation you just demonstrated you don't need.
6. **Every node is exitable to its claim.** One link, always visible, to the
   registry entry the atom exists to make readable.
7. **No account, no email, no cookie, no analytics beyond the platform's own.**
   State lives in the URL and in `localStorage`, and the page must render
   correctly with neither.
8. **Static and no-JS-survivable.** With JavaScript off, every node renders as a
   readable page with the device instruction, the question, all options, and
   every answer's readout inline. The interactivity is an enhancement, never
   the content. Non-negotiable under `SITE-001` — keyboard focus, reduced
   motion, no-JS rendering, and state never encoded by colour alone.
9. **Generated, never hand-edited.** `devices.yaml` → `scripts/generate_foundations.py`
   → `/foundations/`, with a `--check` drift gate like the other nine
   generators. If a node is edited by hand, CI fails. Same rule as every other
   page on this site; no exception for the friendly one.

**The smoothness is rules 1, 2, 5 and 7, not animation.** No wall of text, no
setup, no signup, no waiting through something you already know. The thing that
makes an interface feel like butter is that it never makes you sit through what
you already have.

## 6. The session spine

Per node, six steps. Steps 2 and 3 are the program; everything else is support.

1. **Intention.** One sentence naming what "got it" looks like — a restatable
   limit, not a topic.
2. **Device first.** Hands move. No exposition.
3. **Hinge.** One concept, four options, every distractor a named misconception,
   answerable in a minute, readable in thirty seconds, lucky-correct made hard.
4. **Branch on the answer.** Skip if held. Repair the *specific* misconception if
   not. Never explain the concept generically after a wrong answer.
5. **They write the non-claim.** Not dictated to them. The nearest false thing a
   hostile reader would hear.
6. **Locator.** One link to the claim, and the atom is closed.
7. **Device removed.** Same hinge, no object present, later in the session.
   Added by loophole L8. Passing with the object and failing without it means
   the understanding bound to the beads rather than the structure — a **device**
   failure, recorded against the device, not against the person.

Step 5 is the load-bearer for the whole ethic. A learner who can restate the
finding but not what it fails to license has been inflated, not taught.

## 7. Prompts (token-efficient, for future agents)

Terse on purpose. Each does one job. Substitute bracketed slots.

```
DEV-GEN   Concept [C], estimand [E]. Propose a household manipulable whose
          reachable configurations equal E's feasible set. State the mapping
          explicitly. Name what it cannot show. <=150w.

DEV-AUDIT Device [D], feasible set [S]. Find either (a) a reachable config
          not in S, or (b) a member of S with no reachable config. Report the
          search you ran. Never output FAITHFUL without showing the search.

LADDER    Device [D] against N1-N6 in docs/foundations/PROGRAM.md §2. Output
          FAITHFUL | ILLUSTRATIVE | DEMONSTRATION and the failing criteria by
          number. Do not argue a device up a rung.

HINGE     Concept [C]. Four options, one legal. Each distractor names a real
          misconception -- label which. Answerable 60s, readable 30s. A lucky
          correct answer must be hard.

BRANCH    Learner chose [X] on hinge [H]. Name what X identifies about their
          model, then the single next move. Do not explain C.

STRIP     Rewrite [S] using no term coined in this repo and no term needing a
          definition. Keep every numeral and locator. If a numeral cannot
          survive the strip, say which and stop.

BIND      For each numeral in [T] emit numeral -> locator (claim id or
          file:key). List unbindable numerals and stop. Do not guess.

KILL      Sentence [S]. Write the nearest false sentence a hostile reader will
          hear. One line. That is the non-claim to publish alongside it.

COUNT     Recount FAITHFUL devices in devices.yaml. Presentations of one device
          count once. Report the number and every rung change since last run.
```

`DEV-AUDIT`'s last clause is the one that matters. An audit that returns
FAITHFUL without exhibiting its search is not an audit, and it is the exact
failure mode of an agent that wants to be helpful.

## 11. Machine-checkable faithfulness — the upgrade that matters

In the physical world N2 is *audited* by a person trying to build an illegal
configuration with their hands. That is honest and it does not scale, and it is
exactly the kind of check this repository normally refuses to leave to judgment.

**A device implemented as a state machine can have its faithfulness proved.**

Define a device digitally as three things:

1. `S` — the state encoding (e.g. for D-002: which coins are in the overlap).
2. `moves(s)` — the manipulations the interface permits from state `s`.
3. `feasible(θ)` — the estimand's feasible set as an executable predicate.

Then reachability is a graph search from the initial state, and faithfulness is
a set equality:

```
reachable(S₀, moves)  ==  { encode(w) : w ∈ feasible(θ) }
```

Both directions are the two halves of the §2 falsifier, and both are decidable
when `S` is finite. `D-002` has 11 states. `D-007` has C(8,4) = 70 arrangements
of which 2 are the parity sets. `D-006`'s cell has 3 values. These are trivially
enumerable.

**Proposed: `scripts/audit_device.py --device D-002`.** Exits 0 when the two
sets are equal; on failure it prints the witness — the reachable-but-infeasible
configuration, or the feasible-but-unreachable world. **That witness is the
falsifier, produced automatically.**

This is the move that makes the whole program belong here rather than on a
teaching blog: the device spec stops being a rubric and becomes a verifier, with
a `--check` gate like the other nine. A device whose audit script does not exit
0 is not FAITHFUL, and no amount of enthusiasm about the object changes that.

It also collapses L4 for digital devices — the audit is not a person's opinion,
so designer and auditor being the same human stops mattering. **Physical-only
devices keep the SELF-AUDITED ceiling until a second person tries to break
them.** That asymmetry is correct and should stay visible.

Caution, stated so it cannot be lost: **a machine-checked faithfulness proof is
a statement about the object, not about a learner.** It says the interface
cannot lie about the structure. It says nothing about whether anyone understood
it. Do not let a green check become evidence of teaching.

## 12. The Blender lane

Blender is present on this host (`/Applications/Blender.app`). It is not
installed as a CLI. Four uses, ordered by how much they earn their place, and
one refusal.

### 12.1 Fabrication — highest value, lowest risk

Blender is a modeller, so the physical devices become printable files.

- **D-007, the Bead Cube.** A transparent 2×2×2 shell with eight seated wells
  and four beads. This is the device that most needs to physically exist,
  because its whole argument is *look at the far corner*, and a drawing lets the
  viewer suspect the drawing.
- **D-004, the Sorting Line.** Twenty objects with an **exact, designed** size
  ordering. A printed set has ground-truth ranks; a handful of household objects
  only approximately does. Printing makes D-004 exactly faithful rather than
  approximately, which is the difference between the top rung and the second.
- **D-002.** Coins already work. Do not print coins.

Ship as STL plus a one-page card. Files, not a kit, not a product — K3 stands.

### 12.2 Renders whose visuals are computed, not drawn

The existing films are HTML/CSS renders bound to `films/data/facts.json`. That
binding makes the *numerals* checkable. It does not make the *pictures*
checkable — a diagram of three shadows is an assertion by whoever drew it.

Blender changes that for one specific class of image:

> **Render D-007's three faces as actual orthographic projections of the bead
> geometry.** The shadows are then computed by the renderer from the arrangement,
> not drawn by a person. If the two arrangements really do cast identical pairwise
> shadows, the render proves it; if they do not, the render cannot be made to lie
> without editing the geometry, which the receipt would catch.

That is a genuine epistemic upgrade to the film pipeline and it is small: one
`.blend`, two arrangements, six orthographic cameras. **A picture that is a
projection is bound in a way a picture that is an illustration never is.**

The same applies to nothing else in the current slate, which is why this is one
scene and not a rendering programme.

### 12.3 Render receipts extend to 3D

`films/*/renders/*.receipt.json` already hashes outputs. The 3D lane needs three
additions and no new protocol: the `.blend` file's sha256, the Blender build
string, and a fixed sampling seed with `--cycles-device CPU` for determinism.

```bash
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b scene.blend -f 1 -- --seed 0
```

Two renders of the same `.blend` on the same build must be byte-identical or the
scene is not receipt-eligible. Verify that before modelling anything, because a
non-deterministic renderer cannot enter this repository's film pipeline at all.

### 12.4 Interactive devices — where Blender stops

Model in Blender, export glTF, **drive it from a small deterministic state
machine in the page.** Not from Blender.

The reason is §11. If the reachable configurations are defined by geometry nodes
or physics inside a `.blend`, the reachable set is opaque and cannot be
enumerated, so `audit_device.py` has nothing to check and N2 becomes a matter of
trust. If the reachable set is defined in code, faithfulness is provable and the
mesh is only appearance.

**Blender owns how it looks. Code owns what is reachable.** That line is the
whole design, and crossing it costs the program its only real guarantee.

### 12.5 The refusal

No physics simulation, no soft bodies, no particle systems, no "risk landscape"
flythrough. A simulated visual has an arrangement space nobody enumerated, which
is `DEMONSTRATION` at best and Swiss cheese at worst — a picture that looks like
evidence because it moves. The temptation is real and it is exactly proportional
to how good Blender is at it.

## 13. Where the form might travel

Recorded as ideation, claimed as nothing. **Untested outside this repository.**

The pattern is general: *wherever a published summary leaves a quantity only
partially identified, a faithful manipulable can exist, and its faithfulness can
be machine-checked.* Guardrail composition is one instance.

Candidates a future agent might examine, in rough order of how well the shape
fits:

- **Bounds on treatment effects under non-compliance or missing outcomes.** The
  Manski tradition is partial identification in its canonical form; the strips
  device may transfer directly.
- **Survey non-response.** The overlap device with an unobserved stratum.
- **Diagnostic test combination in medicine.** Two tests' sensitivities do not
  fix their joint miss rate — structurally the same sentence as MC-003, with a
  reader population that already meets it in practice.
- **Ecological inference.** Marginals of a table without the interior.

**Non-claims for this section:** none of these has been attempted; no expert in
any of those fields has been consulted; the transfer may fail for reasons
obvious to a practitioner and invisible here; and this list must not appear on
any public page as evidence that the method generalises. It is a note for
whoever reads this file next.
