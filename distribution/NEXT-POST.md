## Root post

Two checks each miss 10 of 100 faulty cases.

Different misses: together they miss 0.
Same misses: together they miss 10.

Same separate scores. Different protection.

This can happen in a tiny workflow or a large system.

Constructed example. Show the overlap.

## Source reply

This example assumes both checks see the same cases and either can catch a fault. If one check changes the next check’s workload, measure that workflow too.

The example and current research corrections: https://cubits11.github.io/overlap/

## Scope

“Check” can mean a validator, test, review, sensor, classifier, or a subsystem producing a binary decision. The example concerns a defined shared pool of faulty cases and an either-check-catches composition rule. It does not give a blanket reliability result for adaptive, routed, stateful, or arbitrary multi-stage systems. Valid-case false alarms, latency, and cost need separate measurement.

No E6 range, E7B confirmation, real-world frequency, vendor comparison, or new-theorem claim appears in this post. The two counts are constructed. The teaching destination preserves credit for established probability bounds.
