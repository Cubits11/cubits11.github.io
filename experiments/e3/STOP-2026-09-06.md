## 2. What the kill rule says to do

> *"If the disjointness assertion fails … E3 stops and reports the stop. It does
> not proceed with a widened target."*

This file is that report. The gate did exactly what it was written to do, and it
did it **before** any download, any inference, any boundary change — which is
the entire argument for writing gates before experiments rather than after.

The error being caught is real and it is in `PREREG.md` itself. Written
2026-09-03, it says E3 draws from pools *"already pinned and hash-verified"* in
E2's `sources.json`, and never checks whether E2 had left anything in them. It
had not. **A preregistration written three days ago was refuted by its own first
assertion, at zero cost.**
